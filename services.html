Our Services

Six ways we can
support your project.

From collecting inputs and defining requirements all the way through test planning and staff augmentation — Stratum Engineering provides whatever your project needs, whenever you need it. Every engagement is handled personally, with first-principles rigor at every step.

01 / Design
Design & Documentation
From collecting inputs and defining requirements, to drafting and documentation, to product creation and delivery — Stratum Engineering is here to support your design-related needs from beginning to end. We produce clear, stamped deliverables that stand up to review.
02 / Analysis
Engineering Analysis
Specializing in stress, fatigue, seismic, thermal, and fluid flow analyses. Offering a full suite of analysis methods, Stratum Engineering prides itself on checking all analyses through a rigorous application of first principles — reducing risk and preventing costly surprises downstream.
03 / Consulting
Technical Consulting
In challenging project environments, you need solutions before they impact your cost and schedule. We pride ourselves on quickly developing impactful solutions that are creative, effective, and simple to implement — bringing an outside perspective when it matters most.
04 / Project Management
Project Management
Managing resources effectively to optimize their allocation is mission critical to a project's success. Through a strict process-oriented approach, we plan, manage, and execute your projects through to completion — on time, on scope, and on budget.
05 / Staff Augmentation
Staff Augmentation
Stratum Engineering provides highly skilled engineers on demand, seamlessly integrating with your team to support project surges, specialized tasks, or long-term initiatives. Our flexible staffing solutions ensure you have the right expertise at the right time — reducing overhead while maintaining project momentum.
06 / Test Planning
Test Planning & Validation
We develop comprehensive test plans tailored to your project's needs — ensuring rigorous validation, regulatory compliance, and operational reliability. From defining test objectives to executing structured protocols, we help mitigate risk and streamline development cycles.
